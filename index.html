Frequently Asked Questions — Palm Beach County Electrician

Answers to the most common electrical questions from Palm Beach County homeowners and businesses.

How much does an electrical panel upgrade cost in Palm Beach County?
Most residential panel upgrades in Palm Beach County run between $2,500 and $4,500 depending on amperage (typically 200-amp service), accessibility, brand of panel installed (Siemens, Square D, or Eaton), and whether any service-mast or meter work is required. Federal Pacific or Zinsco replacements may cost more if extensive rewiring is needed. We provide free written estimates on all panel work.
Do I need a permit for an electrical panel replacement in Florida?
Yes — Florida requires a permit for all electrical panel replacements and service upgrades. Gator Electric handles the entire permit process with the Palm Beach County Building Department, including scheduling the final inspection. You never have to deal with the county yourself.
What is a Federal Pacific panel and why is it dangerous?
Federal Pacific Stab-Lok panels were installed in millions of homes from the 1950s through the 1980s. Independent testing has shown that the breakers fail to trip during overloads at significantly higher rates than other brands, creating a fire hazard. Many insurance companies in Florida now refuse to write or renew policies on homes with Federal Pacific panels. Replacement is strongly recommended.
How long does a panel replacement take?
A typical residential panel replacement takes one day. Power is off for 4–6 hours during the swap. We coordinate with FPL for any service drop work, pull the permit beforehand, and schedule the county inspection — usually within 1–2 business days of completion.
Does Gator Electric offer financing for panel upgrades?
Yes — we offer financing options for panel upgrades, whole-home rewiring, and EV charger installations. Ask during your free estimate and we'll walk you through the options that fit your budget.
Is Gator Electric licensed and insured in Florida?
Yes. Gator Electric is a fully licensed Florida Electrical Contractor — License #EC0002289 — and we carry full general liability and workers' compensation insurance on every job. Certificates of insurance are available on request.
What cities does Gator Electric serve in Palm Beach County?
We serve all 36 municipalities in Palm Beach County — including Lake Worth Beach, West Palm Beach, Boynton Beach, Delray Beach, Boca Raton, Wellington, Palm Beach Gardens, Jupiter, Royal Palm Beach, Greenacres, and every smaller city and unincorporated area in between.
What types of electrical work does Gator Electric handle?
We handle residential and commercial electrical work — panel repairs, upgrades, and replacements; whole-home rewiring; outlet, switch, and ceiling fan installation; lighting; EV charger installations; solar panel wiring; generator hookups; commercial build-outs; fire pump controllers; LED retrofits; and 24/7 emergency electrical service.
